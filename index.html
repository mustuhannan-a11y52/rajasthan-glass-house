Meet Our Owner

The Vision Behind Our Growth

Abbas Kanchwala — Owner, Rajasthan Glass House

Abbas Kanchwala has led Rajasthan Glass House since 1998, when he took the reins from his father and set out to build something bigger.

With courage and dedication, he grew the family’s framing business into a glass enterprise, contributing to the growth of Banswara’s glass industry. He is known for his relationships with customers and his commitment to quality.

Community-minded and generous, he values family, wants to help others grow, and looks to carry the family’s values on to the next generation.

“True success is not only about growing a business, but also about growing together with the people who believe in it.”A guiding belief of Rajasthan Glass House, not a direct quotation.
